# Picture time

The **picture time** limits how long photos are looked at per day. You find it under **Playtime › Picture time** while the photo display is on.

It only counts while an album is open on the display. The [screensaver](bildschirmschoner.md) does not count.

## Own limit or listening time

| Choice | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Own limit** | photos have a daily budget of their own, apart from the listening time |
| **Counts on the listening time** | there is no picture limit of its own; when the listening time is used up, the photos take a break too |

With **Own limit**:

- **Own daily limit** on: enter the minutes for each weekday (20 at first). Off: no limit for photos.
- **+10 min**, **Free today** and **Reset** apply to today only, like the [instant actions](../spielzeit/schlaftimer.md) of the listening time.
- A new day starts at the same hour as for the [daily limits](../spielzeit/tageslimits.md).

## Warning and idle

| Setting | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Warning before the end** | the display says so 2, 5 or 10 minutes before (or not at all) |
| **Pause when the grid is idle** | when an album lies open and nobody taps, the time stops counting after 1, 2 or 5 minutes |

When the picture time is used up, the display shows **The pictures are taking a break**. Audio books and music go on. Quiet times apply to photos too.

On the app's start page, the **Today** tile shows two small bars when there is a picture time of its own: one for listening, one for pictures.
