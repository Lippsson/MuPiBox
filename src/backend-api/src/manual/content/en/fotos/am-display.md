# Photos on the display

When the photo display is on and at least one album is visible, the display has the **Photos** tab.

## Albums

Every album is a small stack of its first pictures with its name below.

- **Tapping the stack** opens the album.
- **Tapping the name** reads it aloud, as for audio books and music. So children who cannot read yet find their album too.

Which albums appear on the display and in which order you decide in the app under **Library › Photos** ([Uploading photos](hochladen.md#managing-albums)).

## In an album

The album shows its pictures as a grid. Clips carry a small label with their length. Tapping a picture opens it large.

In the large view:

| Button | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Arrows left and right (or swipe) | previous and next picture |
| **Slideshow** (play at the bottom centre) | starts or pauses the slideshow |
| Speedometer | speed: slow, medium, fast |
| Frame | **Show picture large** (fills the display) or **Show whole picture** |
| Arrow top left | back to the album or to the albums |

The buttons hide after a short while; a tap brings them back. A clip has a button for pause and play in the middle.

With **Album starts as a slideshow**, an album starts with the slideshow straight away ([Switching on and settings](einschalten.md)).

## Picture time

While a picture time of its own runs, a small label in the corner shows the minutes left. Shortly before the end a warning appears ("5 more minutes of pictures"). When the picture time is used up, the display shows **The pictures are taking a break** with a button to the audio books. Audio books and music go on as long as there is listening time left ([Picture time](bildzeit.md)).

When the box locks (listening time, quiet time, "quiet now") or the display goes off, the slideshow and a clip stop. They go on only when someone taps again.

## Not there right now

Albums shown live from Immich or Synology Photos need the network. If the service cannot be reached, such an album turns grey and shows **Not there right now**. **Try again** asks again at once. Albums kept on the box are not affected ([Photo services: keep or live](speichern-oder-live.md)).
