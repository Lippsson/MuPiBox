# NAS folders

Folders on your NAS can become photo albums. The box uses the connection set up under **Library › NAS** ([NAS](../inhalte/nas.md)). The choice of photo folders is separate from the folders for audio books and music.

Under **Library › Photos › Connect a photo service › NAS folders**:

1. Tick the folders in the tree that are to become albums. Every folder chosen becomes an album.
2. **Subfolders as albums of their own**: on, every subfolder becomes an album of its own; off, photos from subfolders go into the album of the folder.
3. Save. The sync starts at once. An album appears as soon as its first photo is on the box.

The box fetches the photos in display size to the memory card. So they are there even when the NAS sleeps or is off. It takes videos along when **Show videos** is on.

| Setting | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Sync › Interval** | how often the box looks for new, changed and deleted photos (every 15 minutes to daily, or only by hand) |
| **Cache › Use at most** | how much room the photos from the NAS may take (500 MB to 8 GB, or no limit) |

**Sync now** looks at once. **Stop using the folders** removes the albums from the NAS from the box. Everything stays on the NAS.

> [!NOTE]
> If the box finds no photos in the folders chosen, read rights are usually missing: the box's account needs them on the NAS for the folders **and** for the files in them.
