# Uploading photos

In the app under **Library › Photos**, tap **Upload photos**.

1. Under **Where to?** choose an **Existing album** or create a **New album**. The **Album name** is what the display shows (at most 40 characters).
2. Pick the files with **Choose photos & videos**; on a PC you can also drag them onto the area.
3. The box checks first whether there is enough room on the memory card. If not, it says how much is missing and uploads nothing.
4. A bar shows the progress. Keep the page open until everything is uploaded.

| What | Limits |
| --- | --- |
| Photos | JPEG, PNG, WebP and HEIC (iPhone), up to 50 MB per file |
| Videos | MP4, MOV and M4V, up to 1 GB and **3 minutes**; only with **Show videos** ([Switching on and settings](einschalten.md#videos)) |

The box keeps every photo only in display size (about 1280 pixels) and without location data. The original stays on your phone. Videos are brought to a size the display can play, in the background.

## Managing albums

Under **Library › Photos** every album has a switch **Visible on the display**. Hidden albums stay on the box but do not appear on the display. **Order** sets the order of the albums on the display.

Tapping an album opens it in the app:

- **Add photos** uploads more photos into this album.
- **Select** marks several photos to delete them.
- Tapping a photo shows it large; there **Use as cover** puts the picture on top of the stack, **Delete** removes it.
- **Rename** and **Delete album** are there for uploaded albums.

Albums from the NAS, Immich or Synology Photos are managed where the photos are. In the app they can be shown or hidden and refreshed with **Sync now**.

## Backup

The **Full backup** (**Settings › System › Backup**) contains the uploaded photos and videos with their albums. Photos from the NAS, Immich and Synology Photos are not in it; the box fetches them again after a restore ([Backup](../wartung/backup.md)).
