# Photos

The box also shows photos and short videos on the display: as **albums** the child opens, and if you like as a **screensaver** when nobody has used the box for a while. Looking at pictures has a **picture time** of its own, apart from the listening time.

The photo display is off at first. You switch it on under **Settings › Photos** ([Switching on and settings](einschalten.md)).

## Where the photos come from

| Source | How | Page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Upload** | photos and videos from a phone or PC into an album on the box | [Uploading photos](hochladen.md) |
| **NAS folders** | folders of the NAS the box is connected to become albums | [NAS folders](nas-ordner.md) |
| **Immich** | albums of an Immich server in the home network | [Immich](immich.md) |
| **Synology Photos** | albums from Synology Photos on a Synology NAS | [Synology Photos](synology-photos.md) |
| **Nextcloud** | albums of the Nextcloud Photos app | [Nextcloud](nextcloud.md) |
| **PhotoPrism** | albums of a PhotoPrism in the home network | [PhotoPrism](photoprism.md) |
| **iCloud** | shared albums from the iPhone, by link | [iCloud](icloud.md) |

The box can keep albums from Immich, Synology Photos, Nextcloud, PhotoPrism and iCloud or fetch them live when they are shown ([Photo services: keep or live](speichern-oder-live.md)).

## Getting started

1. Open **Settings › Photos** and switch on **Photo display**.
2. In the app under **Library › Photos**, tap **Upload photos**, or set up a source with **Connect a photo service**.
3. The display gets the **Photos** tab as soon as an album is visible ([Photos on the display](am-display.md)).
4. Under **Playtime › Picture time** you decide how long photos may be looked at per day ([Picture time](bildzeit.md)).

> [!NOTE]
> Photos stay on the box in your home network. There are no links to share. The box only loads photos from your home network or from the services you set up yourself.

If something does not work, see [When photos are missing](probleme.md).
