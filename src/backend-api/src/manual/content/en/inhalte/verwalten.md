# Managing content

Everything on the box is managed on the **Library** page: filtered by category and source, grouped by artist. A tap on an artist shows its entries, a tap on an entry opens it for editing.

## Artist subscriptions

An **artist subscription** fetches all albums of an artist, such as a series of radio plays ([Spotify](spotify.md)). The **Subscriptions** tile at the top of the library shows everything that was subscribed or added via the search.

A tap on a subscribed artist shows the **Subscription** section above its episodes:

- **Episode from / Episode to**: only a range of episodes, for example 1 to 80. “Episode” is the position by release date, 1 is the oldest. Empty means open. **Apply range** saves the range.
- **Include/exclude albums** shows all albums of the artist with a switch. Switch off means: this album does not come onto the box through this subscription.
- **End subscription** cancels the subscription and takes its albums off the box at once. Albums from one of your Spotify playlists and albums added one by one stay.

A subscription none of whose episodes is on the box yet is listed in the library all the same (“no episodes yet”).

## Individual albums

An album added on its own is opened in the library like any entry. **Remove** takes it off the box; it does not come back with the next sync.

## Do not forget the backup

The list of content, the covers and the configuration are in the **configuration backup** ([Backup](../wartung/backup.md)). Make one before larger changes.
