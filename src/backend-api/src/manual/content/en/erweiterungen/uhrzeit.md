# Time in the header

The extension **Time in the header** shows the time at the top of the display, to the right of the battery indicator. It sets up nothing on the box and needs no accessories.

After [installing](installieren.md) it, its settings are in the details of its card under **Settings › System › Extensions**:

| Setting | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Display style** | **Digital** (digits) or **Analog** (a small clock face) |
| **Format** | **24 hours** or **12 hours**. The face of the analog clock always has 12 hours |

A change takes effect at once; the display reloads briefly for it.

> [!NOTE]
> The box has no clock of its own. It gets the time from the network. Without a network it runs on with the time it last saved and can therefore be wrong until it is online again.
